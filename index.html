About Meg

Where psychology
meets creativity

Hello, I'm Meg. I create scroll-stopping short-form content with strategy behind every frame.

My background is a little different. I hold a First-Class Psychology degree, which means I do not just make content that looks good. I understand why people stop scrolling, what earns their trust, and what moves them to act. Every hook, caption and call to action is built on how audiences actually think.

I sharpened that foundation with a 12-week digital marketing course at Another Avenue, covering strategy, content, audience growth, personal branding and analytics. My final project, a campaign launch for YOMA retreats, scored 87, a high pass.

Content that feels real. Strategy that performs. No corporate jargon, no hype, just work made to convert.

Away from the edit suite I am a qualified yoga teacher and I co-host women's wellness retreats in Thailand. That side of my life keeps my content human, grounded and authentic, and it is exactly why wellness, travel and lifestyle brands feel at home working with me.
