Secondary · Poppins Uppercase Lockup
  • Primary wordmark is the name set in Kaushan Script, always in Flame (#ff5a2e), on Blush, Cream or White backgrounds. On the sunset gradient, use White.
  • Secondary lockup is Poppins 800 uppercase with wide letter-spacing, used where script would be too decorative (footers, small print, dark surfaces).
  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the capital "M" free on all sides of either mark.
  • Do not stretch, outline, add drop shadows to, recolour outside the palette, or set the wordmark on busy photography without a scrim.
  • Never pair the wordmark with any other brand's logo or colours.

04 · Typography

Two typefaces, one voice

Display & Script Accent
Kaushan Script
Use for: her name, hero flourishes and single accent words inside headings. Never for: body copy, buttons, captions or anything long. Keep it rare so it stays special.
Hero name: 52 to 84px · Section flourish: 28 to 40px
Headings, Body & UI
Poppins carries everything else

Weights 400 to 800. Clear, friendly and modern. Body copy sits at 15 to 16px with relaxed line height.

Eyebrow labels: Poppins 600, uppercase, 2.5px letter-spacing, Flame, ~12px. Headings: Poppins 700 or 800 in Ink.
H1: 32 to 44px 800 · H2: 24 to 28px 700 · Body: 15 to 16px 400 · Caption: 12 to 13px 500
05 · Voice & Tone

How the brand speaks

Warm, authentic, confident, human. Wellness-calm but results-aware. Speaks plainly, no corporate jargon, no hype. Sounds like a sharp, friendly professional who understands people. Short lines. One idea at a time.

We say

"Let's make content people actually stop scrolling for."

"Every post has a job. Here's what this one does."

"Real moments work harder than polished ads."

We don't say

"Leveraging synergistic omnichannel solutions."

"Go viral overnight, guaranteed!"

"Best-in-class disruptive growth hacking."

07 · Imagery Style

What the brand looks like

  • Sunsets and golden-hour warmth. Light that feels like the palette.
  • Travel, wellness and real-life moments. Lived-in, not staged.
  • Authentic UGC-style photography and video. Phones welcome, people first.
  • Photo frames: 3 to 4px solid Flame or Coral borders with a slight rotation, echoing a collage feel.
  • No stock-corporate imagery. No handshakes in glass offices, no posed suits.
Framed photo treatment: Flame or Coral border, slight tilt
08 · Quick Rules

Do and don't

Do

  • Lead with Flame and the sunset gradient.
  • Set body text in Ink on Cream, White or Blush.
  • Keep Kaushan Script for the name and rare accents only.
  • Use pill buttons: Flame or sunset fill, white Poppins 600 text.
  • Use real photos and honest, plain language.
  • Leave placeholders where a real proof point is still to come.

Don't

  • No green or gold anywhere in the identity.
  • No ForeShiloh logos, colours or straplines. This is Meg's brand.
  • No invented metrics, testimonials, client names or results.
  • No em dashes or double hyphens in copy.
  • No corporate jargon, hype or stock-corporate imagery.
  • No light grey paragraphs on cream backgrounds.
